Tips for round GIFs

Leave room for motion

Move the circle over the face or logo, then watch a full loop. If a moving detail crosses the edge, reposition the circle or make it larger before exporting.

Check how transparent corners look

The downloaded GIF has equal width and height. The corners are transparent, so the background behind the GIF shows through. Check that the app or site where you plan to use it supports animated GIFs and transparency.

Frequently asked questions

Does my GIF leave my device?

No. This tool processes GIFs in your browser. Your image is not sent to a server.

Will the animation keep playing?

Successful exports keep the original frame count, frame delays, and loop setting.

Why does a circle GIF still have square dimensions?

The GIF has a square canvas. Pixels outside the circle are transparent, so your background shows through the corners.

What does Square mode do?

Choose Square to crop a 1:1 GIF without applying a circular mask. Choose Circle when you want the corners outside the circle to be transparent.

Can the colors change?

GIF frames have limited palette space. When the circle needs an extra transparency entry, the tool may adjust some colors and will show a notice next to the result.

Can I crop a rectangle?

Use the freeform GIF cropper for a rectangle with independent width and height.

What if a GIF cannot be cropped?

Some GIFs use frame structures this version cannot preserve for a particular crop. Try a different crop area or another file.

Which browsers should I use?

Tested on desktop Chrome, Edge, and Firefox, and on Android Chrome. Use an up-to-date browser with JavaScript and WebAssembly enabled.

Supported files and browsers

GIF files only. Current limits: 20 MiB per file, 4,194,304 canvas pixels, 500 frames, and 60,000,000 total frame pixels. All limits apply together. Processing stops after 60 seconds. These are protective limits, not a guarantee that every GIF within them will work on every device.
