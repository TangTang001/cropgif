Frequently asked questions

Can I compress a GIF without changing its appearance?

Start with the default settings. They optimize how the GIF stores its frames while keeping the original dimensions, colors, and frame count.

What happens when I remove frames?

Motion may look less smooth. The tool adds the skipped frame delays to the frames it keeps and preserves the GIF's loop setting.

Why is the result larger?

An already optimized GIF can grow when re-encoded. Keep the original if the result is larger, or try fewer colors, smaller dimensions, or fewer frames.

What if I need an exact size?

Use Resize GIF to set a specific width or height.

Does my GIF leave my device?

Your browser compresses the GIF locally. Your file stays on your device.

Supported files and sizes

Choose a GIF up to 20 MiB and 500 frames. Both the original and the compressed GIF must fit within 4,194,304 pixels per canvas and 60,000,000 pixels across all frames. If the output exceeds these limits, choose a smaller size.
